MONSTARS — Interactive Musical Playground

Project Overview

Monstars is an interactive musical playground that transforms music-making into a playful, hands-on experience for children. Featuring five quirky musical characters and a colourful three-level stage, Monstars invites children to build their own little orchestra by simply dragging, dropping and experimenting.

The idea is simple: you don't need to understand music to start making it. Through movement, sound and animation, children can explore rhythm, layering and musical expression at their own pace.

Design Scenario & Response

Monstars was designed to make musical exploration feel approachable, intuitive and rewarding for younger audiences.

Children naturally learn through play, so I wanted to create an experience that encourages curiosity rather than requiring instructions or prior musical knowledge.

Each character has its own musical personality, and every interaction produces an immediate response. By turning musical controls into a colourful, interactive environment, Monstars allows children to discover how different sounds work together through experimentation.

Core Interactive Features

Drag, Drop & Play

Children can bring five musical characters onto the stage to build their orchestra. Each character introduces a different sound, encouraging them to explore new combinations.

Three Levels of Musical Expression

The staircase acts as an interactive sound controller. Moving characters between its three levels changes their volume, tone and animation speed, allowing children to discover how placement influences sound.

A Living Orchestra

Characters come to life through rhythmic animations, changing colours and playful visual effects. These responses make musical interactions feel immediate, expressive and rewarding.

Play at Your Own Pace

Individual sound toggles, Pause All, Resume All and Reset controls give children the freedom to experiment, make changes and start again whenever they like.

Extended Technique

Monstars explores spatial sound manipulation, transforming traditional audio controls into a simple drag-and-drop interaction.

Using JavaScript and the Web Audio API, each staircase level applies different volume and low pass filtering settings. CSS animations also respond to character placement, creating a connection between what children see, hear and do.

This allows musical concepts to be explored through physical interaction rather than technical controls.

Design System

Monstars uses bold colours, expressive characters and playful typography to create a welcoming environment for younger users.

The yellow staircase establishes a clear interactive focal point, while character lighting, animations and musical notes communicate when actions are successful.

Responsive layouts adapt the experience across different screen sizes, with testing conducted in Chrome and Firefox. Reduced-motion support and clear visual feedback also informed the design.

Every visual and interactive decision was made to support one goal: make music feel inviting, understandable and fun to explore.

Final Reflection

Monstars demonstrates how playful interaction can make musical exploration more accessible.

By giving children the freedom to discover sound through movement and experimentation, the instrument encourages creativity without demanding technical knowledge.

Ultimately, I wanted to create something that doesn't just let children play music, but makes them excited to explore what music can become.

Development and Final Commit History

Monstars was developed using HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with the Web Audio API managing sound manipulation and JavaScript handling character interactions and playback synchronisation.

The final development stage focused on responsive layouts, cross-0browser testing and maintaining consistent interactions across different screen sizes.
